[Termius](https://termius.com/) provides a secure, reliable, and collaborative SSH client.

We use Termius to access our project agents from a phone: checking progress, giving focused assignments, reviewing results, and returning to ongoing terminal sessions. PeopleBot supplies the project workflow and durable context around that work.

Read [Talk to your agents from your phone](/tutorials/termius-mobile) for the practical tutorial.

The Termius name and official logo belong to Termius. Their appearance here is a proposed acknowledgment, not an endorsement of PeopleBot.

## Applying for support

The [Termius open-source program](https://termius.com/opensource) offers eligible teams one year of its Team plan. This is plan access, not a cash-credit award.

The team-account owner applies with a proposed logo, description, and link placement. The program requires noncommercial use, a core-contributor applicant, and an actively maintained project at least 30 days old under a permissive open-source license. Approval is discretionary.

**Eligibility needs clarification:** PeopleBot is licensed GPL-3.0-only, a copyleft license rather than a permissive license. Ask Termius whether it can accept the project before claiming eligibility. This page does not change PeopleBot's license.
